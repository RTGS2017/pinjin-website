## Renze machinery and rubber-plastic parks

In Renze District, Xingtai, the machinery-manufacturing cluster and the rubber-plastic new-materials cluster both passed 10 billion yuan in revenue last year. For January–October this year the page lists machinery-manufacturing revenue of 92.17亿元 and rubber-plastic new-materials revenue of 88.90亿元.

On 1 November, phase one of Xingwan Intelligent Manufacturing Cloud Park in Renze had all 20 modular standard factory buildings occupied.

Guo Jianbo, operations manager of the park, is quoted: Xingjiawan Town has many machinery firms; the park uses unified planning and build-to-order factories. Phase two is not yet under construction, and the planned 24 buildings are already fully reserved.

Renze is pushing Xingwan Intelligent Manufacturing Cloud Park, Xingwan Machinery and Hardware Industrial Park, and Tiankou Rubber-Plastic Industrial Park, and is gathering firms into those parks.
