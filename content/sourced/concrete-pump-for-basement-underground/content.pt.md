## Energia e ar — o que não está no catálogo

Electric 15 and Electric 20 are 380V 50Hz trifásico. If the porão has no grid, that is a site fact, not a Pinjin indoor-diesel rating. Diesel rows (for example Diesel 30 at 2000 kg) belong in the [no-electricity diesel](/blog/diesel-concrete-pump-no-electricity/) discussion — outdoors, with exhaust handled by the buyer’s code. We will not invent an air-change rate.

ACI 304.2R covers placing by pump. It does not turn Electric 15’s 15–20 m vertical cell into a two-level porão guarantee. Pipeline equivalent length still applies — [high-rise selection](/blog/high-rise-building-concrete-pump-selection/) explains vertical vs horizontal cells without calling a porão a tower.

## Mistura e distância

B500S is a pedra fina / mortar-range row (10 mm, 5 m / 5 m). A coarse-stone porão slab that needs 60 m of line is not that row. Electric 15 lists 8–10 m³/h and 60–80 m horizontal. Those are catalogue figures, not a pour-duration promise.

Prime the line before concrete — [priming](/blog/concrete-pump-priming-grout-lubrication/). A dry line in a tight stairwell still plugs.
