## Hopper litres are not m³/h

Marketplace listings often put hopper capacity next to output as if they were the same number. On our Синтай tables they are two rows. The hopper is the receiving box under the grate. Theoretical output is a separate m³/h line. Alibaba’s pump-machine buying guide tells buyers to look at hopper size and valve type; we agree it is a **spec dimension to read**, not a slogan to copy.

We не выдумывать a “keep the hopper two-thirds full” rule as a Pinjin specification. Industry notes (including ACPA hopper warnings) belong in the safety section below.

## Mixer pump: drum series versus pump hopper

The [integrated mixer pump](/products/integrated-mixer-pump/) lists hopper 0.4 m³, output 21 m³/h, and a series note: 30 series with 400 mixer; 40/50 series with 500 mixer. That 400/500 line is how we pair mixing drums on the same trailer. It is not a 0.4 m³ versus 0.5 m³ hopper upgrade story, and it is not a batching plant. ## Hopper safety we will not skip

ACPA’s mechanics procedures warn: never work in the hopper or on the agitator unless the engine is shut off and accumulator pressure is verified at zero. We do not add invented lockout brands or Синтай-only hour meters. If you send photos of grate damage, send the model slug with them.

## What to send with an inquiry

Send mix, max stone, target m³/h, pipe H/V, and whether you are looking at a pump hopper or a mixer-pump drum. Do not send only “need 0.6 m³ hopper” copied from a listing — we will still open the output and distance rows.
