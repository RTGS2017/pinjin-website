## Mini is a search term — we match millimetres

Marketplace pages sell “mini concrete pump” as a title. Hebei Pinjin Machinery does not list a model called Mini. For narrow and confined construction sites we put the published envelopes on one table and ask whether the gate, corridor or indoor slab can take that width and weight. Equating “mini” with Electric 15, Electric 10 Series or B500S-83D would be false: they are different machines.

B500S-83D carries a short boom on the compact unit. That boom is not a truck-mounted placing boom. Its 5 m / 5 m window is indoor or courtyard conveying of sand and 5–16 mm stone, not a substitute for Electric 15’s pipeline row.

Electric 20 is already 2800 × 1300 × 1500 mm and 900 kg. Electric 30 / Low Pressure 40 are 3300 × 1500 × 1500 mm and 1200 kg. If those frames will not pass the gate, do not order them and hope the word mini still applies.

## Confined-site questions we actually use

- Gate and path width versus the millimetre row.
- Floor loading: we publish machine weight, not a floor-load certificate.
- Mix: 5–16 mm belongs on B500S-83D. 1–3 cm belongs on Electric 15 or the rural diesel compact. Do not feed Electric 15 stone into the two-stage 5–16 mm unit.
- Power: 380 V for B500S and the electric compact line; 17 kW diesel when there is no grid ([diesel without electricity](/blog/diesel-concrete-pump-no-electricity/)).
- Reach: if the pour face is beyond 5 m and you were looking at B500S-83D, stop and read Electric 15 or Electric 10 Series instead.
