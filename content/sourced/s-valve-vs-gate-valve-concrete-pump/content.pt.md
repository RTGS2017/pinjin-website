## Industry class, not a Pinjin shoot-out

ACI 304.2R §4.2 describes hydraulically powered directional valves on piston pumps, and separately ball-valve pumps. We cite the section so the words “valve type” have an industry home. We do not publish a factory white-paper that ranks tubo S against slide-gate for every mix. We also do not list a ball-valve trailer.
