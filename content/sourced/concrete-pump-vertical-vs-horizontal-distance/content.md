## Read the pair, not a slogan

“Max conveying distance” on a marketplace card is often one number. Our Xingtai pages print a **horizontal / vertical pair**, and on several electrics they split that pair by aggregate. We do not add H + V into one metre and then pick a model. We also do not treat Electric 80’s 900 m horizontal as a licence to claim Electric 60’s 600 m vertical on the same job.

This page is not a substitute for the [high-rise floor-to-metres note](/blog/high-rise-building-concrete-pump-selection/). That article converts storeys. This one explains why the two columns exist.

## Catalogue pairs we are allowed to quote

| Model | Output | Pressure | Printed distance |
| Electric 15 | 8–10 m³/h | (height/distance listed, no MPa cell) | 60–80 m H / 15–20 m V |
| Electric 40 | 21 m³/h | 23 MPa | Fine-stone 120 m / 360 m; agg13 40 m / 120 m |
| Electric 60 | 40 m³/h | 35 MPa | Fine-stone 200 m / 600 m; 13 mm 150 m / 450 m; 24 mm 100 m / 300 m |
| Electric 80 | 60 m³/h | 40 MPa | 900 m / 300 m (≤2 cm) |
| Diesel 50 | 30 m³/h | 30 MPa | 150 m / 450 m |
| Diesel 120 | 100 m³/h | 40 MPa | 150 m / 500 m |

Fine-stone versus 13 mm versus 24 mm on Electric 50 / 60 / Low Pressure 60 are **different jobs**. Copying the fine-stone vertical onto a 24 mm mix is how people get a blockage discussion. See [pressure, slump and aggregate](/blog/concrete-pumping-pressure-slump-aggregate/).

## What industry text says that we will not turn into a Pinjin formula

ACI 304.2R (and the ACPA note it cites) records estimating equivalents: vertical rise adds pressure, elbows add equivalent length, and a metre of rubber hose is not a metre of steel. We cite that so buyers stop treating hose runs as free. We do **not** publish a Xingtai spreadsheet that converts your drawing into guaranteed bar. If the stated H and V fall inside a printed pair for that aggregate, we discuss that model. If they do not, we say so.

Alibaba buying guides list “maximum horizontal/vertical reach” as a checkbox. Use it as a prompt to open the model table, not as a reason to blend two models.
