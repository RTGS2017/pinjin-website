## Reciprocating engines

A reciprocating engine moves pistons back and forth in cylinders. That is the family the printed 6105 diesel on Diesel 50 belongs to. The chapter does not measure a new bore.

The row stays 99 kW, 30 m³/h, 30 MPa, and 150 m / 450 m for 1 cm aggregate, with the 3300 kg cell already printed beside it. No destination country is added.

## Combustion turbines

A combustion turbine burns fuel in a continuous stream and spins a shaft. The Diesel 50 page does not print a turbine. Do not retitle the 6105 diesel as one.

If the pour needs the 35 m³/h row, that is Diesel 60, printed separately. A turbine chapter is not a bridge between those two rows.

## Wankel engines

A Wankel engine uses a rotor in a housing instead of reciprocating pistons. Nothing printed on Diesel 50 is a rotor displacement.

Keep the engine code as 6105 / 99 kW. The commercial numeral 50 is not a Wankel size and not a pressure.

## Forced induction

Forced induction pushes extra air into a cylinder with a compressor. The Diesel 50 page does not print a boost pressure or a compressor model.

The 30 MPa figure is the concrete outlet cell already on the product page. Leave it there. This chapter adds no price and no jobsite case.
