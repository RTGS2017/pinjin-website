## Trailer pump means pipeline, not boom metres

A 品锦 trailer sits on a frame, takes mix in the hopper, and pushes it through pipe or hose to the pour face. Reach is the **水平 / 垂直 pair printed on that model page**, plus the pipe you actually lay. A 臂架泵’s headline number is usually boom length above the truck. Those are different machines.

ACI 304.2R discusses 拖式泵s and truck-mounted pumps as separate equipment classes. We do not reprint that manual. The factory takeaway is: if your inquiry says “36 m boom”, we cannot match a 品锦 SKU. If it says output, 骨料, pipe length and power, we can open a trailer page.

## Site access is the usual reason people ask

Marketplace copy emphasises that boom trucks need wide roads and outrigger pads, while trailers and mini pumps fit tighter sites. On our floor that matches why 电动15 is 450 kg and 1900 mm long, and why we also list a rural diesel pump at 800 kg.
