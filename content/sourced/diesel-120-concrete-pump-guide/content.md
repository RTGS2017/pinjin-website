## Catalogue Diesel 120 row

Diesel 120 Concrete Pump is a high-capacity diesel trailer concrete pump manufactured by Hebei Pinjin Machinery in Xingtai, Hebei, China. Catalogue parameters: twin 145 kW diesels (290 kW total), 100 m³/h, 40 MPa, 150 m / 500 m. Hopper 0.6 m³, Kawasaki 112 (two units), 7000 × 2000 × 2300 mm, 8000 kg, aggregate 6 cm and below.
