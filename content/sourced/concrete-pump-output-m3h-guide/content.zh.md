## Theoretical m³/h is the cell we will defend

“How many m³/h do I need?” is a site question. The answer we can print is which 邢台 row you are standing on. Catalogue output is theoretical. We do not guarantee site m³/h, and we 不要编造 a floors × thickness formula. Send pour volume, idle time, pipe and mix; we will name a listed band.

## What we still need from the site

- Pour size and how many idle minutes you expect (we will not convert that into a fake efficiency factor).
- Pipe length and stone — a 60 m³/h row does not cancel an over-long 细石 line ([high-rise selection](/blog/high-rise-building-concrete-pump-selection/), [aggregate](/blog/fine-stone-concrete-pump-aggregate-size/)).
- Whether mixing happens on the same trailer (搅拌泵) or the mix arrives separately.
