## Start from the stone on the ticket, not the model number

A 细石 concrete pump is not a separate 品锦 series. It is a trailer whose published max 骨料 and, where printed, 细石 distance pair match the mix. We quote those cells. We 不要编造 a 邢台 formula that converts slump into stone size.

ACI Committee 304’s *Guide to Placing Concrete by Pumping Methods* (ACI 304.2R-17) limits angular coarse 骨料 to one-third of the smallest inside diameter of the pump or pipeline, and well-rounded 骨料 to two-fifths of that diameter. That is industry guidance, cited from ACI’s publication. It is not a 品锦 test sheet and not a reason to ignore the max-骨料 cell on our 产品页.

## ACI diameter check — how we use it

ACI 304.2R-17 also notes that for a small-diameter line, all coarse 骨料 must pass the designated screen; the guide’s example is 1 in (25 mm) or less recommended for 3 in (75 mm) pipeline. We cite that as the industry document. We still require the mix to sit inside our printed max 骨料. Example: 电动20 lists 80 mm pipe and 1–2 cm stone. That is the factory row. We will not “upgrade” it to 3 cm because a search title said 细石.
