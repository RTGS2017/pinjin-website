## Start from the stone on the ticket, not the model number

A fine stone concrete pump is not a separate Pinjin series. It is a trailer whose published max aggregate and, where printed, fine-stone distance pair match the mix. We quote those cells. We do not invent a Xingtai formula that converts slump into stone size.

ACI Committee 304’s *Guide to Placing Concrete by Pumping Methods* (ACI 304.2R-17) limits angular coarse aggregate to one-third of the smallest inside diameter of the pump or pipeline, and well-rounded aggregate to two-fifths of that diameter. That is industry guidance, cited from ACI’s publication. It is not a Pinjin test sheet and not a reason to ignore the max-aggregate cell on our product page.

### Fine-stone pair versus agg13 pair

Electric 30, Low Pressure 40 and Electric 40 print a fine-stone conveying pair. Electric 30 and Electric 40 also print an “aggregate 13” pair. Those are different rows. A mix named fine-stone is not automatically 13 mm. Send the sieve or the largest stone with the inquiry.

## ACI diameter check — how we use it

ACI 304.2R-17 also notes that for a small-diameter line, all coarse aggregate must pass the designated screen; the guide’s example is 1 in (25 mm) or less recommended for 3 in (75 mm) pipeline. We cite that as the industry document. We still require the mix to sit inside our printed max aggregate. Example: Electric 20 lists 80 mm pipe and 1–2 cm stone. That is the factory row. We will not “upgrade” it to 3 cm because a search title said fine-stone.

Pressure and slump are a different page: [Concrete Pumping Pressure, Slump and Aggregate](/blog/concrete-pumping-pressure-slump-aggregate/). Low Pressure 40’s name is explained in the [Low Pressure 40 guide](/blog/low-pressure-40-concrete-pump-guide/).
