## Start from the stone on the ticket, not the model number

A حصى ناعم concrete pump is not a separate Pinjin series. It is a trailer whose published max ركام and, where printed, حصى ناعم distance pair match the mix. We quote those cells. We لا تختلق a شينغتاي formula that converts slump into stone size.

ACI Committee 304’s *Guide to Placing Concrete by Pumping Methods* (ACI 304.2R-17) limits angular coarse ركام to one-third of the smallest inside diameter of the pump or pipeline, and well-rounded ركام to two-fifths of that diameter. That is industry guidance, cited from ACI’s publication. It is not a Pinjin test sheet and not a reason to ignore the max-ركام cell on our صفحة المنتج.

## ACI diameter check — how we use it

ACI 304.2R-17 also notes that for a small-diameter line, all coarse ركام must pass the designated screen; the guide’s example is 1 in (25 mm) or less recommended for 3 in (75 mm) pipeline. We cite that as the industry document. We still require the mix to sit inside our printed max ركام. Example: Electric 20 lists 80 mm pipe and 1–2 cm stone. That is the factory row. We will not “upgrade” it to 3 cm because a search title said حصى ناعم.

Pressure and slump are a different page: [Concrete Pumping Pressure, Slump and Aggregate](/blog/concrete-pumping-pressure-slump-aggregate/). Low Pressure 40’s name is explained in the [Low Pressure 40 guide](/blog/low-pressure-40-concrete-pump-guide/).
