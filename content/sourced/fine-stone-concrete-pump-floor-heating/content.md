## What this page covers — and what it does not

Buyers searching for a fine stone concrete pump for floor heating backfill are usually placing a floor fill or bedding mix that still contains stone, not spraying plaster. At our Xingtai factory we match that job to published trailer-pump rows. We do not design hydronic circuits, pipe spacing or fill thickness. Those numbers belong to the building drawing. What we can check is whether max stone, pipe length and output sit inside a catalogue line.

Marketplace listings for “fine stone concrete pump” vary by supplier and mix the term with mortar sprayers. We do not copy those listings. Our rows are on the product pages.

## Fine-stone is a catalogue row, not a marketing name

On Pinjin electric trailers, “fine-stone” is a published pumping-distance pair. It is not a substitute for reading max aggregate.

| Model | Motor | Output | Fine-stone H / V | Max aggregate | Weight |
| Electric 15 | 15 kW | 8–10 m³/h | not listed as a fine-stone pair; 60–80 m / 15–20 m overall | 1–3 cm | 450 kg |
| Electric 30 | 30 kW | 12–15 m³/h | 60 m / 180 m | ≤ 3 cm | 1200 kg |
| Electric Low Pressure 40 | 37 kW | 12–15 m³/h | 80 m / 240 m | see product page (fine-stone row) | 1200 kg |

Electric 15 also lists small building sites and secondary structure. If the floor pour is short, 8–10 m³/h, and stone stays in 1–3 cm, we discuss that compact unit first. If the mix is called fine-stone and the pipe is longer than Electric 30’s 60 m / 180 m but still inside 80 m / 240 m, we discuss Low Pressure 40. Output on both 30 and LP40 stays 12–15 m³/h — the step is distance and motor, not a jump to Electric 40’s 21 m³/h.

## Floor-heating backfill is still a pumping match

Contractors often place a fine-stone or small-stone fill around floor-heating pipes, then a screed. From the pump side the questions are the same as any indoor pipeline pour:

- What is the largest stone in the mix, not the name of the mix?
- How many metres of pipe, including elbows, to the pour face?
- Is the site on a grid that can feed 15 kW, 30 kW or 37 kW?
- Will the trailer pass the courtyard or stair well (Electric 15 is 1900 × 900 × 1200 mm / 450 kg; Electric 30 and LP40 are 3300 × 1500 × 1500 mm / 1200 kg)?

If the material is mortar or plaster with particle ≤8 mm or ≤4 mm, stop. That is a spraying-machine job on /products/spraying-machines, not this page.
