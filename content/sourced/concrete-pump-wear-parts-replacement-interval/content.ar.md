## ساعات السوق ليست خلية كتالوج شينغتاي

Search “concrete pump قطع التآكل” on Alibaba and many kits print 250–500 hours. That is listing observation. Hebei Pinjin Machinery does not print those hours on [S-valve](/products/concrete-pump-s-tube-seal/), [piston](/products/concrete-pump-split-piston/), [carbide spectacles](/products/carbide-spectacles-plate/) or [rubber hose](/products/concrete-pump-delivery-hose/).

ACPA’s study guide is the inspect/replace rule we will cite: خرطوم النقل — replace at the first الضفيرة المكسورة; do not wait for a burst. Inspect the أنبوب S, pistons, cutting ring, شبكة القادوس and pipe gaskets. Never open a coupling under pressure — [clamp article](/blog/concrete-pump-clamp-coupling/).

This is not a rewrite of [spare parts list](/blog/concrete-pump-spare-parts-wear-parts/), which already maps SKUs.

## ما نسعّره مقابل ما لا نختلقه

- [S-valve](/products/concrete-pump-s-tube-seal/) and [piston](/products/concrete-pump-split-piston/): send photos; model slug.
- [Spectacles plate](/products/carbide-spectacles-plate/): carbide wear face; still no hour cell.

- Hose: ID, length, working pressure — [hose vs steel](/blog/concrete-pump-hose-vs-steel-pipe/).

Daily checks stay on the [maintenance checklist](/blog/concrete-pump-daily-maintenance-checklist/). Mix that cuts pistons faster is a site fact — [aggregate](/blog/fine-stone-concrete-pump-aggregate-size/) — not a reason to copy a 250 h listing.

Pinjin does not print warranty hours, “OEM life” or a carbide hardness number that is not on the صفحة المنتج.
