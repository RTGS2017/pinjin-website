## Floor screed and small slabs: match the mix, then the pump

“Small concrete pump” is a search phrase. In our Синтай catalogue it is not a model name. For floor screed and concrete slab construction we still read four printed rows: output, max заполнитель, conveying distance, trailer size. We не выдумывать screed thickness, drying time or a guaranteed floor m² per hour.

If the floor mix is мелкий камень and the pipe is longer than Electric 30’s мелкий камень pair, Low Pressure 40 is the next printed step at the same 12–15 m³/h. If the mix carries 1–2 cm stone on a short house slab, Electric 20’s 10 MPa / 80 mm pipe row may fit. Electric 15 is the lightest electric trailer in this set (450 kg) when 8–10 m³/h and 1–3 cm stone are enough.

Electric 10 Series lists theoretical 21 m³/h with only 25 m горизонталь and a 0.1 m³ hopper. That is a compact unit, not a slab-rate claim. Read it on its own page.

## Screed versus stone concrete versus spray mortar

- Stone still in the mix → trailer concrete pump (this page).
- Fine-stone floor fill around heating pipes → [Fine Stone Concrete Pump for Floor Heating Backfill](/blog/fine-stone-concrete-pump-floor-heating/).
- Particle ≤8 mm cement mortar or ≤4 mm plaster → [spraying machines](/products/spraying-machines/), not a small прицепной насос.

We do not copy marketplace texts that call every floor machine a “screed pump”.
