## Model and power (the largest specification step)

A compact 电动15 (15 kW, 8–10 m³/h, 450 kg) is not an 电动40 (45 kW, 21 m³/h, 2300 kg) and not an 电动80 (110 kW, 60 m³/h) or Diesel 120 (100 m³/h). Output ladder: [how many m³/h](/blog/concrete-pump-output-m3h-guide/). Diesel versus electric is a different power train ([sites without electricity](/blog/diesel-concrete-pump-no-electricity/)). Name the catalogue slug before asking for a number.

Mixer pumps add a mixing motor on the same trailer (electric: 45 kW + 14 kW). That is not a mixing plant.

## Configuration and 易损件

Quotes move when the inquiry adds pipe length, elbows, clamps, hoses, split pistons or seal kits. Those parts are quote-only, not small-batch parcels, no list price — [spare parts](/blog/concrete-pump-spare-parts-wear-parts/). Confirm DN or OD on the pump.

Voltage/frequency, hopper options and paint or packing notes belong in the inquiry. We 不要编造 a surcharge table.

## Destination, MOQ, Incoterms

- Destination country and port change packing and freight. Freight is the buyer’s cost on this site’s stated terms.
- Quantity: one trailer versus a project batch.
- Incoterms (EXW 邢台 versus other terms) must be on the quote, not assumed from a marketplace card.
- No overseas warehouse and no published dealer list.
