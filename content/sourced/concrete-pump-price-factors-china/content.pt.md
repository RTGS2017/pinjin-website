## There is no list price on this website

Product pages, the FAQ and the spare-parts hub say the same thing: Hebei Pinjin Machinery does not publish a list price. International freight is extra and paid by the buyer. This article names the factors that change a quotation. It does not fill in dollar or yuan figures. Marketplace listing prices vary by supplier, configuration, MOQ and shipping terms; we do not copy them, and they are not Pinjin EXW.

Pinjin EXW would only be quoted from the Xingtai factory after the model is confirmed. Because no EXW number exists on the página do produtos, none appears here.

## Model and power (the largest specification step)

A compact Electric 15 (15 kW, 8–10 m³/h, 450 kg) is not an Electric 40 (45 kW, 21 m³/h, 2300 kg) and not an Electric 80 (110 kW, 60 m³/h) or Diesel 120 (100 m³/h). Output ladder: [how many m³/h](/blog/concrete-pump-output-m3h-guide/). Diesel versus electric is a different power train ([sites without electricity](/blog/diesel-concrete-pump-no-electricity/)). Name the catalogue slug before asking for a number.

Mixer pumps add a mixing motor on the same trailer (electric: 45 kW + 14 kW). That is not a mixing plant.

## Configuration and peças de desgaste

Quotes move when the inquiry adds pipe length, elbows, clamps, hoses, split pistons or seal kits. Those parts are quote-only, not small-batch parcels, no list price — [spare parts](/blog/concrete-pump-spare-parts-wear-parts/). Confirm DN or OD on the pump.

Voltage/frequency, hopper options and paint or packing notes belong in the inquiry. We não invente a surcharge table.

## Destination, MOQ, Incoterms

- Destination country and port change packing and freight. Freight is the buyer’s cost on this site’s stated terms.
- Quantity: one trailer versus a project batch.
- Incoterms (EXW Xingtai versus other terms) must be on the quote, not assumed from a marketplace card.
- No overseas warehouse and no published dealer list.
