## House pours on the compact rows

A “concrete pump for house” search on Alibaba often lands next to 30–60 m³/h advice and mini-pump notes for porãos. Our Xingtai house machines sit **below** that marketplace band. We already have an Electric 15 applications page and a tractor-4100 rural page. This article is the three-way house match: grid compact, tractor, 17 kW diesel — and a warning not to order Electric 80 for a two-storey slab.

If you mix and pump on one trailer, the [integrated mixer pump](/products/integrated-mixer-pump/) lists 45 kW + 14 kW, 21 m³/h, 4500 kg — a different access problem.

Diesel 50 at 30 m³/h / 3300 kg is the number that happens to sit in Alibaba’s 30–60 m³/h house FAQ. That does not make it the default self-build pump. Read [Electric 15 applications](/blog/electric-15-concrete-pump-applications/) and [tractor 4100](/blog/tractor-4100-concrete-pump-rural/) before stretching a mid trailer into a courtyard.

## Basements and gates

The same buying guide files mini pumps under porãos, pools and restricted areas. We match that intent to Electric 15’s footprint and to the [narrow-site](/blog/mini-concrete-pump-narrow-space/) note. We do not list a “porão pump” SKU. Diesel exhaust in a cellar is the contractor’s ventilation job — see [electric vs diesel on grid sites](/blog/electric-vs-diesel-concrete-pump-grid-sites/).

## Consulta

Storeys **and** metres, gate width, power (15 kW / tractor / 17 kW diesel), max stone, pipe length. No list price.
